HOW TO PLAY

Hit 88 MPH

Steer the DeLorean. Bounce the ball into the equalizer bricks. Darker bricks need more hits. Break every EQ brick to warp to the next year. Static blocks are unbreakable and are not required.

Keyboard

← → or A D move. Space launches. P or Esc pauses. M mutes all audio.

Mouse and touch

Move the mouse or drag across the board to steer. Click or tap to launch.

Power-ups

  • Flux Surge: two extra balls and 10 seconds of auto-aim.
  • Laser Grid: a 15-second shield saves one dropped ball.
  • Proton Cannons: 8 seconds of automatic lasers that break bricks.
  • Temporal Hazards fall from hazard bricks. Catching one causes a Tape Jam that shortens the paddle for 8 seconds.

You have three lives. Your best score is saved in this browser. The game pauses when it loses focus.
